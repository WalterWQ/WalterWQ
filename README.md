### 👋 About me

I write software, design circuit boards and fix cars — usually all three at once.

- 🎓 **BEng Software Engineering** with an HNC in Computing (software development & data analytics)
- 🔧 I run my own vehicle repair business, working towards classic & retro car restoration
- ⚡ I design and hand-build my own electronics: ESP32-S3 controllers, mains power boards, KiCad → JLCPCB
- 🖨️ Klipper-based 3D printing, a home lab of rack servers, and too many projects on the go

#### [Blizzard QTech replacement controller](https://github.com/WalterWQ/blizzard-qtech-controller)

An open replacement main board for the SUN (Snap-on) Blizzard QTech2 vehicle air-con service machine — designed from scratch after the original LPC2148 board died.

- ESP32-S3 controller board + separate 230 V power & relay board
- 10 solenoid valves, compressor, vacuum pump, heater and fan
- 5 pressure sensors, refrigerant scale (load cell), current clamps, hardware watchdog
- Plugs straight into the machine's original wiring harness

`ESP32-S3` `KiCad` `C++` `Power electronics`
